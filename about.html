Our story

A pastry restaurant inspired by the dream of someone truly special.

Gelly's Pastry Haven was created from the dream of the love of my life. She has always wanted to have her own pastry shop and restaurant, a place where people can enjoy beautiful desserts, warm bread, good drinks, and a peaceful space to share meaningful moments.

The name Gelly came from her name, making the restaurant more than just a business idea. It represents her passion, her future, and the kind of place she hopes to build with care, creativity, and heart.

Every part of this website is designed to reflect that dream: elegant pastries, a welcoming dining experience, and a brand that feels personal, professional, and memorable.
